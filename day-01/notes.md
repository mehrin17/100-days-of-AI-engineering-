# Day 1 Large language Models

## What I learned

- An LLM stands for Large Language Model.
- It predicts the next token based on the context before it.
- The model uses probabilities when deciding what token could come next.
- A token is a piece of text, while a token limit is a restriction on how many tokens can be processed.
- LLMs can produce text that is highly relevant to the context.

## What confused me

- I initially confused tokens with token limits.
- Feed-forward was difficult to understand.

## My takeaway

I learned more about tokens and how an LLM generates text by making predictions based on possibilities and probabilities. What surprised me the most was how much training goes into these models and how training, pre-training, and tuning contribute to the final model.
